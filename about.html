Our Heritage & Passion

The Story of Lakeview Hilltop

A haven of warmth, panoramic mountain majesty, and soulful hospitality at 5,500 feet atop Kalimpong's celebrated Deolo ridge.

Sanctuary in the Clouds

Rooted in Deolo, Designed for Serenity

Perched atop the tranquil hills of Deolo, Kalimpong, Lakeview Hilltop Homestay was born from a desire to share the timeless magic of the Eastern Himalayas with discerning travelers from across India and the globe.

Here, away from congested town roads and honking traffic, time slows down. The mornings begin with the golden crown of Mount Kanchenjunga glistening through pine canopies, while evenings are warmed by the crackle of our outdoor stone bonfire and the savory aroma of sizzling barbecues.

Whether you are an artist seeking inspiration, a couple looking for romantic seclusion, or a family reconnecting amidst nature, our homestay provides a comfortable, caring home where you are treated as beloved guests.
