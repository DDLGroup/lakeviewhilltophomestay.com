Welcome to Heaven

Where Alpine Mist Meets Heartfelt Himalayan Hospitality

Tucked away in the serene heights of Deolo, Kalimpong, Lakeview Hilltop Homestay offers an idyllic mountain escape far removed from city rush. At an elevation exceeding 5,500 feet, every morning begins with the majestic panorama of Mount Kanchenjunga painted in shades of gold.

Managed with genuine care by hosts Anirban and Bachhu Roy, our homestay combines comfortable wooden rooms, piping-hot geyser baths, home-cooked Bengali and local delicacies, and peaceful outdoor sit-outs overlooking the mist-laden valleys.

Explore The Region

Must-Visit Spots Around Deolo, Kalimpong

Stay at the heart of Kalimpong's most picturesque sightseeing belt.

📍 2 Mins Away

Deolo Park & Hill

The highest point in Kalimpong offering 360-degree views of the Teesta River, Relli valley, and manicured hilltop gardens.

📍 5 Mins Away

Kalimpong Science City

A fun, educational park with outdoor science exhibits, a dinosaur park, and lush panoramic viewpoints.

📍 12 Mins Away

Dr. Graham's Homes

Historic 500-acre colonial school estate built in 1900 with heritage architecture and Scottish-style chapel.

📍 18 Mins Away

Pine View Cactus Nursery

Famous botanical conservatory housing over 1,500 exotic varieties of cacti and succulents gathered globally.

How To Reach

Finding Us in Deolo, Kalimpong

Located just 4 km from Kalimpong town center on the tranquil ridge of Deolo, Lakeview Hilltop Homestay is easily accessible by taxi from New Jalpaiguri (NJP) Railway Station or Bagdogra Airport (IXB).

  • 🚗 From NJP / Bagdogra: 72 km (approx 2.5 - 3 hours scenic mountain drive)
  • 🚗 From Kalimpong Market: 4 km (10-12 mins uphill drive towards Deolo)
  • 🚗 From Darjeeling / Gangtok: Convenient day transit (approx 2.5 hrs)
