Location & Navigation

How To Reach Deolo Hilltop

Transit Options

  • By Train (NJP Railway Station)
    Approx. 75 km away. Pre-paid or private cabs take around 2.5 to 3 hours through scenic Sevoke and Teesta Bazar.
  • By Air (Bagdogra Airport - IXB)
    Approx. 80 km away. Direct taxi connectivity available from the terminal straight to Deolo ridge.
  • From Kalimpong Town
    Just 4 km uphill drive (10 minutes) passing through lush pine trees and serene hill cottages.
